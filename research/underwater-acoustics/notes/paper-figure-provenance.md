# Paper Figure Provenance

## Why this note exists

Three figures generated by this repository also appear in a paper whose
copyright is held by IEEE. This note records which figures those are, which
direction the reuse runs, and how the repository's licensing relates to the
publisher's. It is a provenance and citation record, not a legal opinion.

## Publication of record

> J. M. Cherry, "How Well-Supported Is That Estimate? Uncertainty-Aware Argo
> Float Interpolation," in *Proceedings of OCEANS 2026 Monterey*, 2026.

The published article is © 2026 IEEE. Cite the paper, not this repository,
when referring to the method, the reported accuracy, or the variance
decomposition as published results.

## Which figures, and which direction the reuse runs

The figures originate here. They are produced by the
`## 110 m Paper-Support Charts` section of
[6-sound-speed-uncertainty-product.py](../notebooks/6-sound-speed-uncertainty-product.py),
using the plotting helpers in
[lib/plotting.py](../notebooks/lib/plotting.py), and are embedded as cell
outputs in the paired
[6-sound-speed-uncertainty-product.ipynb](../notebooks/6-sound-speed-uncertainty-product.ipynb).

| Repository export stem | Appears in the paper as |
| --- | --- |
| `sound_speed_teos10_110m` | Fig. 1(a), sound speed at 110 m |
| `w_raw_110m` | Fig. 1(b), observational support `W_raw` |
| `sound_speed_teos10_w_contours_110m` | Fig. 1(c), sound speed with support contours |

Two further exports from the same section, `sigma_sound_speed_teos10_110m` and
`sigma_sound_speed_teos10_w_contours_110m`, are repository-only and are not in
the paper.

The rendered PNG and SVG files themselves are written to the gitignored
notebook `data/charts/` directory and are not tracked. What is tracked is the
code that generates them, plus the notebook cell outputs.

Numeric results are the same story. The interval-coverage figures recorded in
[sound-speed-uncertainty-handoff-report.md](sound-speed-uncertainty-handoff-report.md)
are the research record the paper's Table II was drawn from, not a
transcription back out of the published article.
